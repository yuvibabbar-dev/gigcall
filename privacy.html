Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-15

Who we are

Gigcall is operated by Yugwinderpal Singh ("Gigcall", "we", "us"), of 245 Fenton St, New Westminster, BC V3M 5H8, Canada. For the purposes of the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, Yugwinderpal Singh is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

Canada. We handle personal information under Canada's PIPEDA and British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act. Yugwinderpal Singh is the individual accountable for our compliance (contact details below). You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

What Gigcall does not do

Gigcall contains no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no tracking of you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not access your device's location or your device's address book, and it does not request permission to do either.

Legal bases for processing (EU/UK GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — your account, your tour content, and your subscription, all of which exist to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — crash reports and basic security logging, to keep the service working and secure, balanced against your right to privacy by collecting the minimum described above.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — push notifications, which your device asks you to allow separately, and which you can withdraw at any time in the app or your device settings.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where we are required to keep certain billing records for tax or accounting purposes.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and its content, while active — for as long as you keep the account.
  • Deleting a single item (a schedule item, a contact, a whole tour) — recoverable for 30 days from "Recently deleted" in the app, then permanently removed by an automated nightly job. Attached files are removed from storage the same way.
  • Edit history and change notices. The before-and-after copy behind the in-app History screen is kept 90 days, then permanently deleted; a notice that your edit was overwritten by someone else's more recent one is kept 30 days. The internal log used to deliver a push notification (its summary text and who made the change) is kept for up to 30 days after it is sent, then deleted.
  • Deleting your account — handled immediately: your account, any tour you own that nobody else is still on, your membership on every other tour, your notification preferences, your push token, your subscription-status record, any referrals or rewards on your side as the referrer, and any unread overwritten-change notice addressed to you are all deleted as part of that one request (not held in a 30-day window the way a single deleted item is). If you own a tour — or an organisation — that other people are still active on, you'll be asked to transfer or empty it first, so their day sheets (or the organisation's roster) are not deleted out from under them. See Delete your account for the full detail, including what a few things (purchase records, Sentry events, an edit you made on a tour you've left) outlive the account by.
  • Backups. Our infrastructure providers may keep routine backups for disaster recovery on their own standard schedules; we do not separately publish a fixed backup-retention number here because it is set by that infrastructure rather than by Gigcall's own code, and we recommend confirming current figures with us at gigcall01@gmail.com if this matters to you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, delete, or restrict our use of your personal data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. To exercise any of these:

  • Access and export — the app's "Export my data" feature (account sheet) gives you a copy of your own tour content at any time.
  • Deletion — delete individual items in the app, or your whole account from the account sheet, or by request at gigcall01@gmail.com or https://yuvibabbar-dev.github.io/gigcall/delete-account.html. See Delete your account.
  • Correction — edit your display name, email or tour content directly in the app at any time.
  • Other requests — write to gigcall01@gmail.com or 245 Fenton St, New Westminster, BC V3M 5H8, Canada. We will respond within the time required by applicable law.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

Gigcall is a professional tool for touring and live-event production and is not directed at, or intended for, anyone under 16 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact gigcall01@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

Connections between the app and our processors are encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Sign-in, password-reset and confirmation emails are delivered by Google's mail service like any other email, so they are not end-to-end encrypted. Access to tour content is restricted by row-level security to the people invited onto that specific tour. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we design the service — offline-first, row-level access control, minimal data collection — to limit what there is to protect.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. We will change the effective date above when we do, and for material changes we will make a reasonable effort to notify you in the app or by email.
